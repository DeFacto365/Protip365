Delete Your ProTip365 Account

You can request deletion of your ProTip365 account and associated app data at any time.

Request Deletion

Email support@protip365.com from the email address tied to your account and include “Account deletion” in the subject.

We will verify the request and delete account data required by the app, except information we must keep for legal, tax, security, or fraud-prevention reasons.

What Gets Deleted

  • Profile data tied to your ProTip365 account.
  • Employer, shift, tip, and income records stored for the account.
  • App settings and account-linked preferences where applicable.

Subscriptions

Deleting your ProTip365 account does not cancel App Store or Google Play subscriptions. Manage subscriptions directly through Apple or Google.
