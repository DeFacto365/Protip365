# ProTip365 V4 — Product one-pager

Status: approved Phase I direction
Date: 2026-07-17

## Plan every shift. Know what it actually paid.

ProTip365 is a private, local-first schedule and earnings app for servers, bartenders, and restaurant workers. Combine shifts from multiple employers, record what actually happened, and compare expected hours and earnings with the real result.

## Phase I

- No account, email, login, or password.
- Encrypted records stored locally on one phone.
- Optional six-digit passcode and device biometrics.
- Multiple employers and employer-specific roles/rates.
- Weekly schedule with create, edit, delete, restore, copy-forward, reusable templates, and recurring rules.
- Scheduled start/end versus user-reported actual start/end, with multiple paid or unpaid breaks.
- Direct, pooled, and mixed tips.
- Tip-pool contribution, tip-out paid, and tip-share received.
- User-set estimated deduction rate showing gross earnings, estimated deductions, and Estimated net for budgeting.
- Expected, pending, partial, received, and disputed payouts.
- Missed/cancelled shifts with reason codes.
- Privacy-safe local reminder when a finished shift still needs actuals or a missed/cancelled reason.
- Optional pre-shift reminders, quiet hours, snoozing, and capped repeat reminders.
- Expected-versus-actual statistics by employer and role.
- Weekly goals, basic trends, and best-day/employer comparisons.
- CSV export and encrypted full backup/restore.
- English, Canadian French, and Spanish.

## Privacy promise

Phase I records stay on the device unless the user explicitly exports them. ProTip365 does not require an account and employers cannot access the user's data.

## Primary workflow

1. Choose a language.
2. Optionally enable passcode/biometrics.
3. Add an employer and default hourly rate.
4. Add roles and role-specific rates when needed.
5. Enter, copy, or generate the upcoming schedule from a reusable template.
6. After a shift, confirm or adjust actual start/end and breaks, then enter tips, other income, deduction estimate, and payout details or mark the shift not worked.
7. Review expected versus actual results, weekly goals, and basic trends.
8. Export a report or encrypted backup at any time.
