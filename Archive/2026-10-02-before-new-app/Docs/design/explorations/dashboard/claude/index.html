Open the app to a payoff, not a list.

A hero card answers "how's my week going?" the instant you open it, a timeline spine ties the week together, and shifts that need attention become the obvious next tap — all in the app's own square-cornered, cobalt language, led by expected-vs-actual (the number a clock-in app can't show).
