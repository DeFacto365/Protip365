## Full Description

### Main Description (4000 characters max)

**Plan every shift. Know what it actually paid.**

ProTip365 is a private schedule, tip, and shift tracker for servers, bartenders, waiters, waitresses, and restaurant workers. Combine schedules from multiple employers, compare scheduled and actual hours, and keep clear earnings records without paper slips or messy spreadsheets.

**LOG TIPS IN SECONDS**
- Record direct or pooled tips, tip-pool contributions, tip-outs paid, and tip shares received
- Compare scheduled clock times with actual clock-in and clock-out
- Track employer and role-specific hourly rates
- Add notes for busy shifts, events, sections, or special details
- Keep each shift organized in one place

**TRACK EVERY SHIFT**
- Use a visual calendar for planned and completed shifts
- Copy a shift, an employer schedule, or a full week forward
- Create, edit, delete, restore, or mark a shift missed/cancelled with a reason
- See daily, weekly, monthly, and yearly income summaries
- Compare expected, actual, pending, and received results
- Spot the shifts and days that earn the most

**BUILT FOR SERVICE WORKERS**
- Made for servers, bartenders, restaurant staff, hospitality workers, and tipped professionals
- Manage multiple employers or jobs separately
- Track different pay rates and work patterns
- Use English, Canadian French, or Spanish

**PRIVATE BY DEFAULT**
- No account, email, login, or password is required
- Records are stored locally on your device
- Protect the app with Face ID, Touch ID, biometrics, or a local passcode
- Export CSV reports or create an encrypted ProTip365 backup

**PHASE I PRICING**
- All local features are free for 30 days
- Continue with a USD $19.99 one-time lifetime unlock or the regional equivalent shown by the store
- Existing records remain readable and exportable if the trial expires
- Cloud Sync for multiple devices is coming soon and is not currently available for purchase

ProTip365 is for tipped workers who want one simple answer after every shift: what did I really make?

Download ProTip365 and start tracking your shifts with clear, organized records.
